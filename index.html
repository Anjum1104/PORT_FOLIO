Vision-Based Driver Fatigue
Detection & Instant Alert System

A real-time webcam pipeline that monitors Eye Aspect Ratio and mouth aperture to detect drowsiness and yawning, triggering escalating audio alerts and a simulated safe-stop after 45 seconds of driver unresponsiveness.

Step 01

Capture

Live webcam frame acquisition at real-time frame rates

Step 02

Landmarks

Facial landmark detection via dlib / MediaPipe 68-point model

Step 03

Features

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and mouth aperture ratio computed per frame

Step 04

Assessment

Threshold comparison with configurable sensitivity; consecutive-frame counters

Step 05

Alerts

Multi-stage audio beep escalation; on-screen overlay warnings

Step 06

Fail-safe

Simulated parking protocol triggered after 45 s of unresponsiveness

Detection signals

EAR = ‖p2−p6‖ + ‖p3−p5‖ 2 · ‖p1−p4‖

EAR drops sharply when the eye closes. Sustained EAR below threshold for N consecutive frames triggers a drowsiness event.

Mouth aperture ratio uses the same landmark geometry to detect yawn events independently of eye state.

Driver response

  • Stage 1 Single beep — short drowsiness or first yawn detected
  • Stage 2 Repeated rapid beeps — sustained closure beyond threshold
  • Stage 3 Continuous alarm — critical fatigue, unresponsive driver
  • Fail-safe Simulated safe parking after 45 s without driver response

At a glance

  • Input Live webcam stream
  • Method Geometric landmark ratios
  • Mode Real-time inference
  • Stack Python, OpenCV, dlib / MediaPipe, playsound
